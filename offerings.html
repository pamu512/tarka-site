Questions

Before you write.

Is Tarka open-source?

No. Source-available under Elastic License 2.0. You can clone, modify, and run it on your own metal or VPC for your own fraud operations. You may not provide Tarka to third parties as a hosted or managed service.

Is there a hosted version?

There is no hosted Tarka Cloud, no SaaS desk, and no resale SKU. The commercial offering is an install pack that helps you self-host. The buyer owns the cluster, the warehouse, and the queue.

How do I become a pilot buyer?

Use the contact form (fastest) or open a GitHub issue titled [commercial] pilot scope request. We'll ask about your stack, your fraud ops team, and your timeline, then send the one-page scope. If the fit isn't right (beta, beachhead, or readiness), we'll say so instead of selling you a pack.

What does it run on?

Docker Compose for the desk (Lite / demo / product skins) and Helm for Kubernetes (prod-on-k8s keeps frontend, desk, and Shadow off by default). Day-1 needs Docker, ~4 GB RAM, and ports 8000/8001/3000/5432/6379 free.

Does the AI decide?

No. The Rust evaluate engine decides from JSON packs. The optional LLM copilot (Advise) is forensics-only. It drafts and explains, never Promotes, never demotes, never decides. There is no Tarka-branded model.

Can I migrate my Drools estate?

There is no Drools / Groovy importer, and the docs don't pretend otherwise. JSON packs are the evaluate plane; migration is parallel / phased cutover only. A buyer assessment framework exists to decide CUT vs BAU honestly.
